# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

AIRSI AlgoTrader is an education and research project. Participants are expected to act professionally, respectfully, and in good faith. Welcome thoughtful technical discussion, disclose uncertainty, distinguish evidence from opinion, and respect different experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, spam, fraud, deceptive performance claims, requests for credentials, and instructions for unauthorized access or live trading without appropriate review.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and the project’s education-only purpose. Security reports and private information should be sent through the process in `SECURITY.md`, not posted publicly.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Concerns should be reported privately to the repository owner through GitHub rather than debated in public.

By participating, you agree to help maintain a safe, respectful, and evidence-oriented project community.
